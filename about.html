About Amber

UCI WorldTour
Cyclist &
Coach.

Born in Katherine, NT, and raised as an athlete, I spent years competing in triathlon before making cycling my full-time focus in 2020. After three consecutive years as SA Female Triathlete of the Year, I made the jump to the UCI Women's WorldTour with Liv AlUla Jayco — racing at the highest level for four years.

My background in Exercise & Sport Science means every program I write is grounded in physiology — not guesswork. I'm extremely passionate about building personalised programs that fit around your life — work, family, and social commitments included.

I've been coaching since 2019, working with beginner through to elite athletes. I'm especially passionate about supporting women and juniors — building confidence, capability, and a genuine love for cycling from the ground up.

I see too many women held back by lack of race skills and confidence, and too many talented young riders following adult programs that lead to burnout. My coaching exists to change both of those things.

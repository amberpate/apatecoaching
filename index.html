How It Works

The Process.

Step 01

Free Consultation

We talk through your goals, history, current fitness, and schedule. Free 30-minute video call — no commitment required.

Step 02

Program Design

A fully personalised program is built around your specific goals, available training time, and target events or race calendar.

Step 03

Train & Review

You train, I monitor. Fortnightly calls, data analysis, and ongoing plan adjustments keep you progressing consistently.

Step 04

Race Ready

Targeted race prep, taper strategy, and on-event support so you arrive confident and ready to perform your best.
